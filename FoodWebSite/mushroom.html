My Amazing Mushrooms

These delicious stuffed mushrooms are pretty easy to make and taste just like the ones you'd get in a restaurant!

Ingredients

  • 12 whole fresh mushrooms
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 (8-ounce) package cream cheese, softened
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon onion powder
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cayenne pepper

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Coat a baking sheet with cooking spray.
  2. Wipe mushrooms with a damp paper towel; carefully remove stems. Chop stems extremely fine, discarding tough stem ends.
  3. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add chopped mushroom stems and garlic; cook until any moisture has evaporated, taking care not to burn garlic. Set aside to cool.
  4. Stir in cream cheese, Parmesan cheese, black pepper, onion powder, and cayenne pepper; the mixture will be very thick.
  5. Fill each mushroom cap with a generous amount of cream cheese filling using a teaspoon. Arrange mushroom caps on the prepared baking sheet.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until mushrooms and filling are piping hot, about 20 minutes.
